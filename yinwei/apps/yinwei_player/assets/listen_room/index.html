Control with your hand

Yinwei uses the front camera while hand gestures are on. Your iPad recognises your hand locally. Video is never saved or sent, and hand recognition needs no network.

You can turn it off with the hand button. Leaving the room, backgrounding the app or an audio interruption releases the camera. Tap the hand button to resume.
